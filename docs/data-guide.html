数据与显示说明

代表样本

旋转小鼠来自作者保存的三维处理体。扫描小鼠使用 70 个位置的 BP 重建，将 1.44–5.76 MHz 与 0.5–8 MHz 两个三维体等权相加。小鼠脑、背侧全躯干小鼠与肝脏直接使用 3D-PanoPACT 官方 v3 发布的 Fig. 3、Fig. 5d 和 Fig. 2a 数值三维体。肝脏为单次曝光重建。叶片、人手与方形靶仿体使用 20 次曝光的 BP 重建。三种视图均由对应 MAT/H5 数组生成，共用 XYZ 裁剪范围和线性窗。

官方来源

Xuanhao Wang、Yuqian Meng、Xiali Gao：3D-PanoPACT_Data_Code v3，CC BY 4.0。相关论文：Nature Communications 16, 10077 (2025)。网站对官方三维体进行轴重排、统一裁剪、线性显示及重新渲染。

颜色、强度与坐标

所有深度 MIP 使用 3D-PanoPACT Trunk 配色，色条表示当前 Z 层范围内的相对位置 0–1。每个样本使用独立线性强度窗，γ=1；网站不再施加额外背景遮罩或亮度变换。灰度 MIP 展示强度，三维视图为斜向最大强度体投影。

时序读数

用户在 MIP 上绘制、移动或缩放区域，曲线实时更新。各时刻先经 Z 轴旋转和 XY 平移配准至首帧，网页使用整段时序共用标定的 8 位线性 MIP 预览，均值取自所有时刻共同有效的像素。每条曲线使用同一组选定像素，相对强度为 I(t)/I(0)。显示窗和比例尺不参与计算。

比例尺与编号

八个样本的比例尺固定在展示框右下角，按实际图像缩放；01 概览只展示图像。记录了原生坐标的数据按坐标计算。官方鼠脑的显示网格约为 0.04 mm，由与本地 BP 结构配准转移；官方全躯干约为 0.0833 mm，由原稿 Fig. 5d 的 5 mm 比例尺转移。肝脏显示网格约为 0.0564 mm，由 Fig. 2a 的 5 mm 比例尺和三维包围框投影标定。三者按各向同性显示网格渲染，详细校准依据见元数据。官方发布体使用 3DPanoPACT-Fig3、3DPanoPACT-Fig5d 和 3DPanoPACT-Fig2a 唯一编号，其余样本沿用已知采集时间编号。

采样与播放

全身序列展示 16 个采样时刻，覆盖 9.8 min；腹部 800 nm 序列展示前 20 个时刻，覆盖 19.23 min。播放按采样帧加速，曲线点保留实际时间间隔。曲线连接相邻测量值，不进行模型拟合。

Data and display guide

Representative specimens

The rotation mouse uses an author-saved processed volume. The scanned mouse combines 70 positions and sums BP volumes reconstructed with 1.44–5.76 MHz and 0.5–8 MHz passbands. Brain, dorsal whole-trunk mouse and liver views use the official 3D-PanoPACT v3 numerical volumes from Fig. 3, Fig. 5d and Fig. 2a. Liver is a single-exposure reconstruction. Leaf, hand and square-target phantom use 20-exposure BP reconstructions. All views are generated from the corresponding MAT/H5 arrays with one common XYZ crop and linear window.

Official sources

Xuanhao Wang, Yuqian Meng and Xiali Gao: 3D-PanoPACT_Data_Code v3, CC BY 4.0. Article: Nature Communications 16, 10077 (2025). Website adaptations comprise axis reordering, common cropping, linear display and re-rendering.

Colour, intensity and coordinates

All depth MIPs use the 3D-PanoPACT Trunk palette. The colour bar denotes relative position, 0–1, within each displayed Z slab. Each specimen has an independent linear intensity window, with gamma 1; the website adds no background mask or brightness transform. Grayscale MIPs represent intensity; 3D views use oblique maximum-intensity ray casting.

Temporal readouts

Draw, move or resize a region on the MIP to update its trace in real time. Frames are aligned to the first frame by Z rotation and XY translation. The web preview uses registered MIPs with one shared 8-bit linear calibration per series and pixels valid at every timepoint, with the same selected pixels throughout each trace. Relative intensity is I(t)/I(0). Display windowing and scale-bar annotations do not enter the calculation.

Scales and specimen IDs

Sample scales remain anchored at the lower right of the preview frame and track actual image scaling; Section 01 shows images only. Recorded native coordinates determine scales where available. The official brain display grid is approximately 0.04 mm, transferred through registration to the local BP grid. The official trunk grid is approximately 0.0833 mm, transferred from the manuscript Fig. 5d 5-mm bar. The liver display grid is approximately 0.0564 mm, calibrated through the Fig. 2a 5-mm bar and volume-box projection. These use an isotropic display-grid assumption; metadata records calibration details. Official volumes use unique IDs 3DPanoPACT-Fig3, 3DPanoPACT-Fig5d and 3DPanoPACT-Fig2a; other specimens retain known acquisition timestamps.

Sampling and playback

The whole-body series displays 16 sampled timepoints over 9.8 min. The abdominal 800 nm series displays its first 20 timepoints over 19.23 min. Playback is accelerated by sampled frame; plotted points retain their actual time spacing. Lines connect adjacent measurements without model fitting.
